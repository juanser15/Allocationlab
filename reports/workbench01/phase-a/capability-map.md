# Workbench-01 Phase A — executable capability map

Checkpoint: `b9950f7cbe128f9d761597a5e216d777cc3059a3`.

This map describes what the committed code can execute. Certification reports are supporting evidence only; classifications come from source entry points, their actual inputs and their persisted outputs. UI-01 JSON and rendering/finalization scripts are `ARTIFACT_VIEW_ONLY` and are not empirical execution evidence.

`PIT_SAFE` is intentionally not assigned without qualification. The empirical engines enforce strict historical cutoffs and next-close execution, but the adjusted-close vendor vintage, historical universe membership and governance vintages are not independently authenticated. The precise label is **PIT mechanics enforced; data-vintage PIT unverified**.

## Interpretation boundaries

- Risky-sleeve weights and final NAV weights are separate outputs.
- Expected beta and realized beta are separate diagnostics.
- `PROMOTED`, `ACTIVE` and `ALLOCATED` are separate bank/pipeline states.
- Gross return and net return remain separate; cost scenarios are explicit.
- Training, inner validation and outer OOS remain separate.
- The empirical runs establish execution mechanics and provenance. They do not establish alpha, profitability, robustness, production readiness or a recommended allocation.
- Renderer/finalizer scripts and UI-01 JSON are `ARTIFACT_VIEW_ONLY` even when the underlying artifact came from an empirical calculation.

The JSON companion contains the unabbreviated commands, inputs, defaults, outputs and evidence paths for each row.
