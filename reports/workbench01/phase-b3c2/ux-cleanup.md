# Primary UX

The configuration remains Backtest Period (From/To), Walk-Forward (Type, Training Window, Rebalance), and one Trading Costs input. Standard mode explicitly maps that input to both selection and OOS costs. No new financial defaults or methodology selector was added. The ordinary UI requires the explicit recurring V2 capability; it fails closed against an old deployment instead of silently running V1.

Model Summary keeps model, decision, information window, selection, recalibration and the concise turnover rule. It says initial implementation still pays costs. Component weights, availability, thresholds, information universe, policy details and execution conventions remain under How the exposure rule works, collapsed initially.

Primary failures show a title, a short plain-language explanation and one next action. Technical class, stage, fold/date, exact candidate evidence, operational counts and diagnostic reference remain available under SHOW TECHNICAL DETAILS, collapsed initially. No dates or training windows are repaired. No candidate-window increase is recommended without verified evidence. Full private worker diagnostics never reach the browser.

Result summaries and fold rows distinguish recurring turnover, total turnover and initial implementation. Training constraint measures and methodology are available under per-fold advanced details. Legacy history remains on its original methodology; rerun reuses its stored experiment identity. A cross-method comparison visibly warns that eligibility definitions differ.

No broker, orders, capital authorization, additional modes or model controls were added. Research/storage/data-vintage limitations remain visible.

A selected training interval with no later actual portfolio change shows a plain-language uninformative-turnover warning. It does not claim demonstrated low turnover. The actual one-month UI execution verifies this warning and its still-costed entry.
