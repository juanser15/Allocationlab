## Implemented boundary and assumptions

- `SIGNAL_RIDGE` is limited to selected B1 mathematical factors → underlying `asset_returns` forecasts → existing capital long-only B2 allocation and B3/A1 historical execution. Alpha-stream/signed-overlay use with this new estimator fails explicitly; old forecast paths remain separate.
- The target is the arithmetic **daily decimal excess return** from the declared execution close to the **next trading close**, net of the cash yield for that same interval. The one-period forecast is not a monthly/quarterly holding-period forecast. Only labels matured by a fit's information cutoff train the model; unobserved outcomes are pending. Live `same_close` supports a declared decision date after the last observed cutoff without accessing future prices; live `next_close` without a known execution trading date fails explicitly.
- One pooled linear model shares an unpenalized intercept and signed slopes across assets. Training-only population standardization, constant-column zero slopes, balanced complete dates, a rolling window of 252 complete dates, minimum `max(60, 5 × (slopes + 1))`, and a finite positive selected `lambdaSignal` apply. B1 factor transformations/direction are kept distinct from regression scaling; exact executable aliases are canonicalized, while different correlated factors remain available.
- Forecast `V_mu` is the **date-aggregated Bartlett HAC sandwich approximation** to sampling uncertainty of the penalized fitted prediction, conditional on the fixed penalty and fitted feature transform. Frozen integer bandwidth defaults to five; the dated score covariance retains full cross-asset terms and missing calendar gaps. It excludes shrinkage bias, omitted-model/regime uncertainty and future return noise; it is neither return-risk Sigma nor a calibrated future-return prediction interval. B2 adds deterministic daily cash once to the excess prediction to publish gross `muHat` without changing `V_mu` or independently estimated Sigma. D4 and optional D5 SIMPLE consume the existing solver paths.

Analytical example (T1): standardized feature values `−1,+1`, corresponding labels `−0.02,+0.02`, and `lambdaSignal=1` give an unpenalized intercept of **0** and a standardized slope of **0.01**. The focused mathematical tests also compare a nonconstant multifeature penalized sandwich against an independent calculation.
