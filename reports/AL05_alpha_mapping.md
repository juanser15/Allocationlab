## D1 is a strategy-return risk model, not an asset factor mapper

`d1-factor-model.ts:51` defines `D1StatisticalFactorRiskModel` on the **original candidate/strategy axis**. For T×N aligned daily returns: S is centered sample covariance (T−1); R is correlation; omega = diag(stream σ)·V_K is **N strategies × K statistical factors**; phi contains retained correlation eigenvalues; xi is N×N diagonal specific-return variance with an explicit producer floor; gamma = omega·phi·omega′ + xi is **N×N strategy-return covariance**. Gamma is a matrix, distinct from the scalar mean-variance risk-aversion gamma. `factorExposure=omega′a` is a return-loading exposure, not capital or asset-market beta.

**D1 may safely supply Sigma_strategy in principle.** AL-04's rejection of an asset-factor adaptation does not disqualify its original strategy-return covariance. Certification requires the original instance IDs/order, correct daily/net/per-capital convention, historical model version, strict prior decision cutoff, source availability, input/config hashes and the explicit specific-floor methodology. The bare D1 object lacks these PIT envelopes; B2 `riskModel.provenance` at `allocation-b2.ts:700` adds candidate IDs, dates, cutoff, prepared-input/dataset identity, but its call at lines 617–626 feeds **gross** return observations. That current call is not an AL-05 NET covariance artifact. Do not merely relabel gamma as net; require a certified externally supplied NET producer artifact or an explicit certified return-convention conversion. No such adapter was added.

D1 diagnostic names `factorCovarianceEigenvalues` / effective rank describe the reconstructed **gamma** stream covariance, not phi. AL-05 labels return covariance effective rank separately from underlying asset risk and never turns statistical-factor loadings into H.
