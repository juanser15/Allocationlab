D2 — SIGNED ALPHA OVERLAYS, GROSS/NET EXPOSURE & LEVERAGE

CLOSED-SCOPE TASK

Stages A, B, C1, C2, C3 and D1 are CLOSED.

GOAL

Extend ALPHA-STREAM optimization so the PM may choose between:

A. CAPITAL_LONG_ONLY
   Existing B2 behavior.

B. SIGNED_OVERLAY
   New D2 behavior.

SIGNED_OVERLAY allows positive or negative alpha coefficients and
user-controlled leverage while preserving:

- B1 alpha definitions;
- B2 optimization architecture;
- D1 risk models;
- B3 point-in-time execution;
- A1 accounting;
- immutable persistence/replay.

D2 must NOT implement:

- cost-aware optimization;
- nonlinear impact/capacity;
- raw-signal coefficient optimization;
- new alpha selection policies;
- PCA as alpha selection;
- new regime logic;
- new factor models.

--------------------------------------------------
1. CRITICAL SEMANTIC DISTINCTION
--------------------------------------------------

CAPITAL_LONG_ONLY and SIGNED_OVERLAY are economically different modes.

CAPITAL_LONG_ONLY

Existing variables b_j represent nonnegative capital fractions.

Existing semantics remain unchanged.

SIGNED_OVERLAY

New variables a_j are SIGNED ACTIVE-EXPOSURE COEFFICIENTS.

They are NOT capital shares.

a_j > 0:
take the alpha's active exposure.

a_j < 0:
reverse the alpha's active exposure.

a_j = 0:
no exposure to that alpha.

Do not require:

sum(a_j) = 1

Do not interpret a negative a_j as “negative cash allocation”.

Do not alter historical CAPITAL_LONG_ONLY snapshots.

--------------------------------------------------
2. ACTIVE ALPHA REPRESENTATION
--------------------------------------------------

For every B1 alpha stream j at decision time t:

Let:

H_ij,t

be the risky underlying-asset target exposure of alpha j per unit of
its reference capital.

Let:

d_j,t

be its residual cash fraction in the existing long-only B1 stream.

For the original capital stream:

sum_i H_ij,t + d_j,t = 1

Define the alpha's ACTIVE excess-return stream relative to cash:

r_excess_j,t
=
r_alpha_gross_j,t
-
r_cash,t

This is the return series used for SIGNED_OVERLAY expected-return
estimation.

If cash yield varies through time, subtract the actual declared daily
cash-return series point-in-time.

Do not assume it is zero.

The covariance/risk model in signed mode is estimated on aligned
alpha EXCESS returns.

--------------------------------------------------
3. SIGNED OVERLAY → UNDERLYING ASSETS
--------------------------------------------------

For signed alpha coefficient vector a:

x = H a

where:

x_i = final risky target exposure to underlying asset i.

Then define:

NetAssetExposure
=
sum_i x_i

CashTarget
=
1 - NetAssetExposure

This is the master self-financing identity.

Examples:

If:

x_A = -0.20

then:

CashTarget = 1.20

when A is the only risky exposure.

This represents short-sale proceeds held in cash.

If:

NetAssetExposure > 1

CashTarget becomes negative.

That represents borrowing / financed leverage and is allowed ONLY if
the user's cash constraint permits negative cash.

Do not count an alpha's old internal cash sleeve separately in signed
overlay mode.

Its role is already captured by expressing the stream relative to cash.

This rule prevents double-counting cash.

--------------------------------------------------
4. ECONOMIC IDENTITY CHECK
--------------------------------------------------

For a single alpha whose original B1 stream is:

50% Asset A
50% Cash

its excess return is:

0.5 * (r_A - r_cash)

If signed coefficient:

a = -0.4

then underlying target is:

Asset A = -0.20
Cash = 1.20

Portfolio return must equal:

r_cash
- 0.20 * (r_A - r_cash)

which must also equal:

r_cash
+ a * alpha_excess_return

within numerical tolerance.

This identity must be tested.

--------------------------------------------------
5. EXPOSURE DEFINITIONS
--------------------------------------------------

For signed alpha coefficients a:

ALPHA GROSS

GrossAlphaExposure
=
sum_j |a_j|

ALPHA NET

NetAlphaCoefficient
=
sum_j a_j

This is a diagnostic only.
Do not interpret it automatically as asset net exposure.

For resulting asset targets x:

LongAssetExposure
=
sum_i max(x_i, 0)

ShortAssetExposure
=
sum_i max(-x_i, 0)

GrossAssetExposure
=
LongAssetExposure + ShortAssetExposure

NetAssetExposure
=
LongAssetExposure - ShortAssetExposure

CashTarget
=
1 - NetAssetExposure

Report all six quantities explicitly.

GrossAlphaExposure and GrossAssetExposure are NOT generally equal
because multiple alpha streams can overlap or offset in the same assets.

--------------------------------------------------
6. EXPECTED RETURNS AND RISK
--------------------------------------------------

For SIGNED_OVERLAY:

Estimate alpha expected EXCESS returns:

mu_excess_j

using the existing B2 expected-return estimator applied to the alpha
excess-return histories.

Preserve the user-configured shrinkage methodology.

Do not use standalone Sharpe as expected return.

Risk covariance is estimated from those same aligned excess returns.

Allow the user to select the existing D1/B2 risk estimators:

- SAMPLE
- DIAGONAL_SHRINKAGE
- STATISTICAL_FACTOR

D1 factor risk must work with signed coefficients without changing its
construction methodology.

--------------------------------------------------
7. REGULARIZED MEAN-VARIANCE OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------

Support SIGNED_OVERLAY with the existing regularized mean-variance
framework.

Decision variable:

a

Objective:

minimize

    (gamma / 2) * a' Sigma a
    + (eta / 2) * a' a
    - mu_excess' a

where:

gamma > 0
eta >= 0

No cash-return term is necessary inside the active objective because
mu_excess is defined relative to cash.

The total expected portfolio return display may report:

ExpectedTotalReturn
=
r_cash
+
mu_excess' a

using clearly consistent units.

Do not auto-tune gamma or eta.

--------------------------------------------------
8. MINIMUM VARIANCE IN SIGNED MODE
--------------------------------------------------

Plain:

min a' Sigma a

with no additional requirement has the trivial optimum:

a = 0

Therefore SIGNED_OVERLAY Minimum Variance must require an explicit
user-configured minimum expected excess return:

mu_excess' a >= targetExcessReturn

with:

targetExcessReturn > 0

Then solve:

minimize

    0.5 * a' Sigma a

subject to:

    mu_excess' a >= targetExcessReturn

plus all selected exposure constraints.

If the user requests signed Minimum Variance without a valid positive
targetExcessReturn:

return:

MISSING_TARGET_EXCESS_RETURN

Do not silently force gross exposure.
Do not secretly reuse the long-only budget constraint.

--------------------------------------------------
9. SIGNED CONSTRAINTS
--------------------------------------------------

Support these constraints in SIGNED_OVERLAY:

A. PER-ALPHA SIGNED BOUNDS

lower_j <= a_j <= upper_j

Examples:

-0.25 <= a_j <= 0.25

Bounds are user-configurable.

B. ALPHA GROSS CAP

sum_j |a_j|
<=
GrossAlphaMax

C. ALPHA GROUP GROSS CAPS

For group G:

sum_{j in G} |a_j|
<=
GroupGrossMax_G

D. UNDERLYING ASSET BOUNDS

assetLower_i
<=
x_i
<=
assetUpper_i

where:

x = H a

E. ASSET GROSS CAP

sum_i |x_i|
<=
GrossAssetMax

F. NET ASSET EXPOSURE

NetAssetMin
<=
sum_i x_i
<=
NetAssetMax

G. CASH

CashMin
<=
1 - sum_i x_i
<=
CashMax

All are optional and user-configured.

There is NO universal:
- leverage target;
- gross limit;
- cash range;
- short limit.

The optimizer must require enough constraints to define a bounded
problem.

If the problem is unbounded:

UNBOUNDED

Do not impose hidden defaults to manufacture a solution.

--------------------------------------------------
10. ABSOLUTE-VALUE LINEARIZATION
--------------------------------------------------

Preserve convex QP structure.

Introduce nonnegative auxiliary variables only as required.

For alpha gross:

u_j >= a_j
u_j >= -a_j
u_j >= 0

then:

sum_j u_j <= GrossAlphaMax

For underlying asset gross:

v_i >= x_i
v_i >= -x_i
v_i >= 0

then:

sum_i v_i <= GrossAssetMax

Do not use post-solve absolute-value clipping.

Do not use binary variables.

Do not impose:

sum |a_j| = constant

in D2.

Gross exposure is a CAP, not an equality.

--------------------------------------------------

Auxiliary absolute-value variables exist only to enforce inequalities.

Verify independently after solve:

GrossAlphaExposure

sum |a_j|

--------------------------------------------------
13. D1 RISK DECOMPOSITION WITH SIGNED WEIGHTS
--------------------------------------------------

D1 formulas must remain valid for signed alpha coefficients.

For:

Var_p = a' Gamma a

verify:

Var_p
=
Var_factor + Var_specific

and, when sigma_p > 0:

MRC_i
=
(Gamma a)_i / sigma_p

CRC_i
=
a_i * MRC_i

sum_i CRC_i
=
sigma_p

Individual CRC_i may be negative.

Do not force risk contributions to be positive.

Explain that negative component contribution can occur when a signed
position diversifies portfolio risk.

Do not interpret negative CRC as negative standalone variance.

--------------------------------------------------
14. B3 EXECUTION
--------------------------------------------------

B3 must execute the resulting UNDERLYING target:

x = H a

through A1.

Do not execute alpha coefficients directly.

At every historical decision:

1. reconstruct B1 alpha states point-in-time;
2. estimate excess returns;
3. estimate selected risk model;
4. solve signed B2;
5. compute x = H a;
6. compute target cash = 1 - sum(x);
7. send consolidated underlying targets to A1;
8. net opposing asset trades before charging external costs.

Between executions:
- quantities persist;
- weights drift;
- shorts remain shorts unless traded;
- cash accrues using the existing convention.

Do not calculate realized master-book P&L as:

sum(alpha coefficient * standalone alpha net P&L)

Actual P&L comes from the A1 underlying-asset ledger.

--------------------------------------------------
15. SHORT-SALE ACCOUNTING
--------------------------------------------------

A1/B3 must explicitly support negative underlying quantities in the
signed-overlay path.

For a short asset:

Asset P&L
=
quantity * price change

with quantity < 0.

Short-sale proceeds increase cash at execution.

Covering the short consumes cash.

Transaction costs/slippage apply once to absolute external traded
notional.

Do not introduce:
- borrow fees;
- hard-to-borrow charges;
- dividends on shorts;
- margin interest;
- locate constraints

in D2 unless those data/models already exist.

Display clearly:

“Borrow and financing frictions beyond the declared cash/cost model
are not modeled.”

This is a known D2 limitation, not a reason to fabricate them.

--------------------------------------------------
16. LEVERAGE / CASH ACCOUNTING
--------------------------------------------------

Equity NAV remains the accounting denominator.

At every target:

NetAssetExposure + CashTarget = 1

GrossAssetExposure may exceed 1.

Examples:

Long = 0.80
Short = 0.50

Gross = 1.30
Net = 0.30
Cash = 0.70

or:

Long = 1.30
Short = 0.20

Gross = 1.50
Net = 1.10
Cash = -0.10

The second case requires CashMin <= -0.10.

Do not confuse:
- gross exposure;
- net exposure;
- NAV;
- cash borrowing.
