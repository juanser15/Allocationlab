name: C3 development evidence boundary
description: Why C3 requires an exact C2 universe and separates responsive planning from full replay.
---

C3 compares three redundancy treatments from one complete, frozen C2 development-evidence universe. C2's selection capacity was widened to eight for a real eight-alpha comparison without changing its diagnostics methodology. Do not relax C3's exact universe/source match to work around a smaller C2 selection.

**Why:** Partial C2 evidence cannot support conservative pairwise grouping over all policy candidates. A complete eight-alpha C2 source also lets the real UI preserve a regime-divergent pair while grouping a separate redundant pair.

**How to apply:** When changing selection capacity or fixture size, keep B2 candidate IDs, C2 selected IDs, and C3 input IDs identical. Do not synthesize missing pair evidence.

Development plan previews verify the persisted source hashes but do not execute full C2 replay on every form edit. Persisting a comparison and replaying its saved record do execute full source integrity/replay checks.

**Why:** Full eight-alpha C2 replay took long enough to block interactive planning, while hash-checked previews give responsive feedback without weakening the save/replay gates.

**How to apply:** Keep previews read-only and source-hash-checked. Never remove full C2 replay from C3 save or frozen replay, and never derive frozen selections from evaluation data.
